    Schematic estimates for comparing options at an early stage. Structure cost covers the gravity framing and columns from the firm’s bay study plus an allowance for the lateral system; foundations are not included. Unit costs, schedule durations and carbon factors are placeholders until they are checked. Final costs, schedule and sizes come from design development and contractor pricing.
